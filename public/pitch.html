The insight

You have a loaded gun and no trigger.

Zetamax already owns what every competitor lacks together: a Monkeytype-grade minimalist drill and server-validated anti-cheat scores, ELO, leagues, and per-tag analytics. The fatal flaw is that none of your loops are closed — the assets exist, but nothing fires them.

LOOP 01 · SHARE

Viral cards, no button

You render beautiful Open-Graph share cards at /r/[run_id] and /leagues/[slug].

No screen ever offers a share.
LOOP 02 · RETURN

Daily hook, hidden

The daily puzzle is the single strongest reason to come back tomorrow.

Gated behind sign-in — even to look at the board.
LOOP 03 · CONVERT

Peak intent, dead end

A fresh personal best is the one moment someone would sign in or brag.

Results screen offers only "Drill again."
The wedge

Trading & quant interview prep.

Candidates grinding mental-math speed tests for Jane Street, Optiver, SIG, IMC through the Oct–Feb recruiting season. They already screenshot scores into Discord and WSO, prep in 4–8 week cohorts, and complain that Zetamac's integer-only format isn't the real test. Money already flows. There's a hard deadline. They share natively.

What to cut.

You're a solo builder. Every hour spent here is an hour not closing a loop or planting the wedge.

A Monkeytype theme catalog
Your value is opinionated cohesion. A 90-theme dump dilutes the one thing that separates you from a clone. At most 3–4 curated palettes, much later.
The MATHCOUNTS / AMC audience (for now)
Lower intent, lower willingness-to-pay, doesn't share. Near-zero-cost reuse of the same infra later — but it splits focus off the trading wedge now.
Web push / email re-engagement
Real infra cost, only pays off once a daily habit exists worth reminding people about. Build the streak first; the shield gets most of the value.
Survival / chained-ops / weekly digests
Variety nice-to-haves that compete for solo-builder time with loop-closing. Park them until core retention is proven.
